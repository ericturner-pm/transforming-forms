# Transforming Forms

Build a premium, dark-themed demo website for a British luxury fashion brand that designs transformative garments — clothing that physically converts from one silhouette to another (e.g. a shirt that transforms into a dress). This is a high-end ecommerce concept site: artistic and sophisticated ("quiet luxury"), but it must feel human and inviting, not sterile or gallery-cold. Warmth should come through in the photography style, the copy voice, and small tactile design details — not through bright colour.

AESTHETIC DIRECTION

- Dark theme throughout: deep charcoal/near-black backgrounds (#0A0A0A – #121212), not pure black

- Accent palette: one warm metallic or muted tone (champagne gold, warm bone, soft terracotta-taupe) used sparingly for CTAs and highlights

- Typography: elegant high-contrast serif for headings, clean sans-serif for body — but keep line-lengths comfortable and copy conversational rather than cold/corporate

- Generous whitespace, but break up the minimalism with warmer textural touches: soft film-grain overlay on hero video, subtle fabric-texture background details, rounded corners on cards rather than harsh edges

- Subtle motion: gentle fade/parallax on scroll, soft cross-fades — nothing gimmicky

- Photography direction (important): favour real-feeling lifestyle and on-body imagery over sterile flat-lay product shots — a person mid-movement wearing the garment, natural light or warm studio lighting, genuine expression, hands mid-transformation of the fabric. Avoid stock-photo-generic or overly airbrushed looks; the imagery should feel like an editorial fashion story, not a catalogue

HERO SECTION

- Full-bleed autoplaying looped background video (muted, no controls) showing a model transforming the garment from shirt to dress in real time — this is the centrepiece of the whole site

- Minimal overlay text ("One Garment. Infinite Form.") with a single soft CTA button

- Subtle scroll-down indicator

PAGES / STRUCTURE

1. Home — hero video, brand story teaser, featured "Transform" showcase module, best-selling products, email/waitlist signup

2. Our Story — fuller brand narrative: the founder's inspiration, British craftsmanship, sustainability-through-versatility, a behind-the-scenes/studio imagery section, a short human "meet the maker" element

3. Shop / Collection — full product grid, filterable by category (Shirts, Dresses, "Convertible" tag), large imagery with hover-reveal second angle

4. Individual product page template — gallery with zoom, multiple angles, on-body lifestyle shots, transformation video clip, size/fit guide, care instructions, "Add to Bag", trust details (shipping, returns)

5. How It Transforms — a dedicated interactive/explainer page: step-by-step visual or short video walking through how one garment becomes another, since this is the brand's core innovation and deserves its own space

6. Journal / Lookbook — an editorial-style blog/gallery page with styling stories, seasonal lookbooks, press features — this is what gives the brand a human, ongoing voice

7. Contact / Stockists — simple contact form, social links, press enquiries

8. Footer on all pages — Instagram feed strip, newsletter signup, legal page placeholders (privacy, shipping, returns)

UX REQUIREMENTS

- Fully responsive, mobile-first

- Sticky minimal navbar (logo centred or left, Shop / Our Story / How It Transforms / Journal / Contact), transparent over hero, solid dark on scroll

- Cart drawer (slide-in) rather than a separate page

- Checkout and account pages can be simple placeholders

TONE FOR PLACEHOLDER COPY

Confident and editorial, but warm rather than austere — write as if a real founder is speaking, with personality and story, not generic ecommerce copy. Short sentences, no exclamation marks, no "Shop Now!" filler.

Generate 6-8 sample products (name, price in GBP, one-line description) themed around convertible/transformable garments, and 2-3 sample Journal/Lookbook entries for the editorial page.
